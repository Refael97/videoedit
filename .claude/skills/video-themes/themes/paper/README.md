# Paper: the notebook tutorial

> Read order: **this file** (the editing prompt) → **`language.md`** (how to build *any* new element in this
> style, incl. the five mandatory rules) → `theme.md` (numbers measured on the anchor, contributions).
> Anchor: one creator, one video (15422, "Everything you need to edit your video with AI", 77 s). Rules from
> a single anchor video are marked *(1/1)* in `theme.md`.

## The idea in one sentence
A friendly expert talks to camera while a clean cream notebook page above them **does the steps** —
folders open, files drop in, a cursor picks the right option, a prompt gets highlighted — like a teacher
drawing on graph paper while they explain.

## Who it is for
Step-by-step tutorials and "here is exactly how" reels: setups, workflows, tools, systems. One presenter,
one take, 9:16, 45–90 s. It sells **clarity**: you could pause any frame and copy the step.

## Editing character
- **The page is a desk, not a slide.** Real objects live on it: blue folders, the actual app window, the actual
  prompt, phone thumbnails of real videos, line-drawn props. Nothing decorative, nothing abstract.
- **Every object works.** A folder opens and files drop in; a window gets clicked; a prompt's blanks light up
  yellow; a laptop's screen lifts out and gets labeled. If an object would just sit there, it doesn't belong.
- **Numbered steps carry the structure.** "שלב 1." in orange + a 2–3 word title in ink, top of the page,
  for the whole step. The end recaps all steps around one object.
- **Three layouts, switched by need:** the face full (hook, emotion, a single UI card over the face), split
  (page on top, face below; the default while teaching), the page full (when the drawing needs room).
  Cuts are hard; the page taking the full frame gets a quick paper-white flash.
- **Calm, quick hands.** Objects pop with a small overshoot, park out of the way when the next one arrives,
  connectors draw in dotted orange. About 1.5 actions per second inside the page.

## Text
- **Hook:** two white lines at the top over the face ("Everything you need to / edit your video with AI"),
  with a collage of 3 real video thumbnails cycling under it — never text alone.
- **Captions:** 1–3 words, white, heaviest weight, thick black outline, on the seam in split (47%),
  at 62% on the full face or full page. They swap hard on the words.
- **Labels:** under each object, ink, 40px; the one being talked about is orange.

## Sound
Voice first, a soft low music bed under it, and small UI sounds on almost every action: pop (object in),
click (cursor), key (typing), tick (list items), swish (layout change), ding (a step completed).

## Never
- Grey bars, skeletons, "lorem", fake UI. Show the real window, the real prompt, the real file names.
- Dark or saturated backgrounds on the page (the only dark thing allowed is a real dark output, shown as-is).
- Neon, glow, gradients on objects, 3D camera moves, shaky zooms.
- Logos of other companies; use the theme's generic sparkle tile for "the AI" and real screenshots only when
  the user provides them.
- More than one primary object being worked on at a time.

## How to shoot for this theme
- **Shot:** a medium close-up, chest up, the speaker **centered**, eyes at ~40% of the frame height. The edit
  uses only the bottom half in split, so keep head and hands within the middle band of the frame.
- **Background:** a calm, slightly dark home/office background (kitchen, shelves), out of focus. It contrasts with
  the bright cream page above.
- **Light:** soft front light on the face; the face should read clearly at half-frame size.
- **Audio:** a clip-on mic is fine to show (the anchor holds it). Talk fast and continuously, no long pauses.
- **Avoid:** wide shots (the face gets too small in split), standing up, busy bright backgrounds that compete with
  the page.
