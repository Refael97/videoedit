# Hooks: the glowing word on the chest

> Read order: **this file** (the editing prompt) → **`language.md`** (how to build *any* new element in this
> style, incl. the five mandatory rules) → `theme.md` (numbers measured on the anchor).
> Anchor: one creator, 4 videos (7784 "7 hooks", 28202, 68494, 93864). No contributors.

> **Not night:** hooks lands *one glowing word* on a full-frame speaker in a bright room; night draws a *system* (ladder, rows, curve) as light on a black stage with the speaker in a box.

## The idea in one sentence
A creator talks straight to camera in a bright, warm room, and the **one word that matters lights up on their
chest** like a neon sign; whenever an idea needs to be *shown*, the frame cuts to a black void lit by amber
corners where a real screen, a gold tile or a row of glossy pills does the explaining.

## Who it is for
Social-media growth, content strategy, "N hooks / N steps / the setting nobody uses", creator education.
One presenter, 9:16, 30–70 s. It sells **energy and clarity**: every few seconds a word you can't miss.

## How it differs from night and neon-studio (both dark)
- The **speaker is always full frame** in a bright daylight room — never boxed, never on a dark stage.
- The dark is **pure black with amber light leaking from two opposite corners**, not a dot grid; it is a place you
  cut *to*, not a backdrop the speaker sits in.
- The signature is **typographic**: a heavy glowing word (gradient fill + same-color bloom) on the chest, with a
  small white lead line above it. Color = meaning (gold idea, fire problem, lime action, violet desire, red neon number).
- Objects are **glossy and warm** (gold tiles, candy pills, dark-glass chips), not neon line drawings.

## Editing character
- **Two worlds, hard cuts:** the room (≈60%) and the void (≈30%), plus short cutaways to example videos (≈10%).
  A cut every 3–6 s.
- **One glow word per idea.** It replaces the captions while it is up; it lands *on* its spoken word; it holds
  until the next idea. Never two glow words at once.
- **Lists are chips.** A numbered chip (yellow ring) names the item; a template line in a hand font shows the
  formula with the slot in **[brackets]** glowing yellow.
- **Proof is a view count.** Example videos come with a dark-glass pill (eye + "40.5M") that catches a glint.
- **On the void, things work:** a real settings screen whose switch flips, a gold tile that slowly pushes in, a
  timeline bar that fills, pills that pop in one per word.

## Text
- **Glow title:** lead line 60px white 800 + glow word Heebo/Inter Tight 900 fitted to 55–75% width (120–230px),
  on the chest (63% on the anchor's framing; under the chin on any framing).
- **Captions:** 1–3 words, white 800, 58px, soft shadow, no outline, at 62% (under the chin).
- **Chips** in the condensed face (Oswald / Karantina); **template lines** in the hand face (Comic Neue / Playpen Sans Hebrew).

## Sound
Voice, a soft music bed (-24 dB), and a hit or shimmer on every visual event: `glow` (a glow word ignites), `hit`
(the cut into the void), `pop` (pills, chips, cards), `toggle` (a switch), `glint` (a view count), `tick`, `key`,
`whoosh`/`swish` (cuts), `ding` (a payoff lands).

## Never
- Boxing or shrinking the speaker; a dark grade on the room.
- Two glow words at once; a glow word that isn't a spoken word; more than one color meaning per title.
- Grey placeholder rows on the screens (the anchor blurs some UI: we show the real rows).
- Logos of real platforms (the anchor shows Instagram/TikTok icons — translated: generic glyph tiles).
- Slow fades, rotations, wobble.

## How to shoot for this theme
- **Room:** bright and warm — daylight from a window behind/beside you, a light wall, a chair. The anchor's room is
  white curtains + soft daylight.
- **Wardrobe:** a **dark, plain t-shirt** (black/navy). The glow word sits on your chest; on a white shirt it needs a
  dark pool behind it (`.h-glowt.shade`) and glows less.
- **Shot:** medium shot, centered, chest visible down to mid-torso (the chest is the title's place); head top
  at 25–35% of the frame. A second, tighter angle (or the edit's 1.15–1.2× jump punch) for emphasis beats.
- **Delivery:** fast, pointed sentences with one stressed word each — that word becomes the glow word.
- **Avoid:** close-ups that crop the chest, busy prints on the shirt, hands held in front of the chest for long.
