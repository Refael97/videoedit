# Neon studio: the creator's room, the real tools, one yellow word

> Read order: **this file** (the editing prompt) → **`language.md`** (how to build *any* new element in this
> style, incl. the five mandatory rules) → `theme.md` (numbers measured on the anchor).
> Anchor: one creator, 4 videos (70647 "Claude just killed LinkedIn", 10899, 1191, 49478). No contributors.

## The idea in one sentence
A creator in a dark neon-lit studio talks straight to camera; whenever they name a tool, the **real tool** appears —
a clean card on a light page or a screenshot floating on a blur of its own colors — and the one word that matters
lands in **bright yellow** under the caption.

## Who it is for
"New AI tool" news, tool demos, "how to set up X", hacks, free alternatives. One presenter, 9:16, 20–40 s, fast.
It sells **access**: "this exists, here is what it looks like, comment and I'll send it".

## How it differs from night (both look "dark")
| | neon-studio | night |
|---|---|---|
| Where the dark comes from | the **footage** (a real room with neon), never a drawn stage | a drawn black dot-grid stage |
| Graphics surfaces | **light page** (#f7f5f1) with white UI cards, or an **ambient blur** of the media | neon structures, dark panels |
| Speaker | full frame, or the bottom half (split), no box | in a rounded box, head popping out |
| Captions | white 2–3 words, building word by word with a blur, **yellow keyword line** under them; the CTA keyword in a **thin italic**, huge | one word, white; serif accents |
| Signature | real UI being used (typing, checks, progress), **pixel sprites** landing on the speaker's chest | ladders, curves, rows unlocking |
| Entrance | **blur-in** (a lens pull) | brighten from dark, neon flicker |

## Editing character
- **The room is the brand.** Most of the time it is just the speaker in their studio. Graphics are short, real and
  readable, and then it's back to the face.
- **Real tools, shown clean.** A card on the light page, the real words in it, and something *happens*: a check turns
  green, the text is typed, the bar fills, a phrase gets a yellow highlight, the cursor clicks.
- **Ambient for media.** A screenshot, a site or a b-roll clip floats in a rounded frame over a heavy blur of its own
  colors (top half in split, or full frame).
- **Pixel agents.** When the script counts helpers / checks / features, little pixel sprites land on the chest, each with
  a pixel icon above it and a label under it.
- **Everything blurs in.** Cards, tiles, captions, pills: 0.3 s from 16px blur. No bounces, no wipes.
- **Layouts switch every 1.5–3 s:** face → split → full page → face.

## Text
- **Captions:** 2–3 words, Inter / Heebo 800 white, words appear one by one as spoken (blur-in); the keyword of the beat
  goes on a second line in yellow #f9fc04, bigger (a short single keyword is huge, 190px).
- **On the page:** captions turn ink (#111) at 68%.
- **Page header:** two lines, ink + blue ("מתקינים / **Kowalski**"), 96px 800.
- **CTA:** "תגיבו" + the keyword in thin italic yellow, 190px, which **stays** while the white line above keeps captioning.

## Sound
Voice first, a soft low bed, and quiet UI sounds: `air` (every blur-in), `pop`, `key` (typing), `tick`, `ding` (done),
`whoosh` (layout change), `blip` (a pixel sprite lands), `coin` (sprites checked).

## Never
- A drawn dark background (that's night). If it's dark, it's the room or the ambient blur.
- Grey bars / skeleton UI: every card shows real words (≥26px).
- More than one yellow thing at a time; yellow on anything but the key word / the key line.
- Logos of other companies: the subject is a brand-free tile (a glyph on a color) unless the user supplies screenshots.
- Bounces, rotations, glitch.

## How to shoot for this theme
- **The room:** dark, with **colored practical light** (an LED strip, a neon sign, a monitor glow, a lamp) behind you, out
  of focus. This *is* the look — without it the theme becomes "headline". A **dark top** (black tee) makes the white
  captions and the sprites pop; a white top swallows them.
- **Shot:** chest-up, centered, eyes at ~35–40% of the frame, the chest free (captions and sprites live there, 55–90%).
  Don't hold objects in front of your chest.
- **Light:** one soft key on the face, warm; the room stays dark.
- **Bright footage** (white wall, daylight) works only graded down (`.s-face.grade`) and loses the studio feel.
- **Audio:** clip-on or shotgun; fast, clean delivery, short sentences.
