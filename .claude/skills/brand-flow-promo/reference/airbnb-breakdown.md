# The Airbnb reference, frame by frame (30 s, studied at 5 fps)

Source: a reel by "מתן חיים | Premium AI Creative" (Instagram DffYpX1jh-m), a phone recording of a laptop screen.
The reel itself is not in the repo. Times are approximate.

| Time | What is on screen | What to take from it |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1.5 | A line loops into the logo mark on a pale pink-white backdrop | logo first; one soft brand-tinted backdrop |
| 1.5–3 | A phone with the real app, tilted in 3D; two search fields lift out of it | real UI, taken apart; the device is only a starting point |
| 3–5 | The phone is gone, a calendar card tilts toward the camera, two dates get picked, camera pushes through it | one card at a time, large; push through as the exit |
| 5–7 | "Where to next?" resolves word by word out of blur on plain white | a quiet line resets attention; words blur in one by one |
| 7–9.5 | A real map tilted in perspective, price pins float above it with shadows, only the chosen pin is sharp and brand-coloured | depth of field on a map; the chosen item is the only saturated thing |
| 9.5–11.5 | The pin grows into a full-screen brand-pink card: "Imagine waking up here." | full-screen brand colour; an element becomes the scene |
| 11.5–14 | Photo cards whip in as a row on pink ("Find the one."), then a swarm of blurred photos flies past the camera | whip pans, heavy motion blur, many photos |
| 14–15 | One photo is chosen, grows; the pink wipes away to white from below | the chosen photo is the bridge to the next scene |
| 15–20 | A space of floating things, camera drifting diagonally: title under the photo, a strip of photos with "2/54" counters, "Entire rental unit" + four chips, a rating card counting to 4.71, host chip, location card, "What this place offers" with six icon tiles popping, a large photo flying past | station every 0.6–0.9 s; everything out of focus except the current station; numbers count |
| 20–22 | Price card with the total counting up and a pink "Reserve" button; camera pushes into the button | the price is the last station; push into the call to action |
| 22–24.5 | The button swells into a pink card, a "Confirm and pay" sheet scrolls, camera pushes into "Confirm and pay" | the purchase is shown, not implied |
| 24.5–26.5 | The button becomes a pink card with a spinner, then "Reservation confirmed" with the listing | spinner → check: a small beat that sells the click |
| 26.5–27.5 | The card turns, shrinks to a dot | morph out |
| 27.5–30 | The logo mark draws out of the dot, the wordmark types in | logo closes the loop |

## Gaps I measured in my own cuts against it
1. Sharp everywhere → depth of field per element.
2. No motion blur → sub-frame accumulation.
3. Small cards, empty frame → main element at 40–60% of the frame.
4. A flat page → objects at different depths with a camera travelling between them.
5. White and one dark colour → a full-screen brand-colour beat and colour in the backdrop.
6. Fades → morphs (pin → card, photo → frame, button → card, card → dot → logo).
7. Holds of 2–3 s → something new every 0.6–1.5 s.
8. Six photos → twenty or more.
9. Ended on a slogan → ends on a confirmed purchase, then the logo.
